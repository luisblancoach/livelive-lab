Brand book

LIVELIVE is an entertainment and eSports medium. Its identity is a typographic reel: four columns, two lines, boxes that change height while the letters keep their construction.

This draft documents the mark, its grid, the fixed safe area, the palette, the height rules and examples of use. Every figure is drawn by LIVELIVE Lab, the instrument that generates the pieces.

Status: working draft generated on . Brand content needs approval. The reference typeface (Akzidenz-Grotesk Condensed) has no confirmed web licence; the Lab draws its own constructed letters.

01 · The markDraft

Four columns, two lines

The mark is LIVE written twice, one line above the other, in four columns. Each column has one brand colour; each box is either a frame (coloured line, white interior, coloured letter) or a block (solid colour, white letter).

The dividing line of every column is the position of a reel. At rest it sits in the middle; in motion it moves, and the boxes change height. The letters never scale: they are built again at every height.

  • Colour order: blue · green · yellow · red.
  • Frame over block is the reference state; inverted, checker, all frames and all blocks are allowed variants.
  • Single line: neighbouring frames share one line. Double line: every frame keeps its four borders.

Safe area rule. Inside every box (a frame's white interior or a whole block) the sign keeps an empty band of 0.20 u above and below it. The measure is the same for every sign, every box and every frame of the animation. The sign lives only between the bands and never touches them.

03 · Height rulesDraft

Letters grow in their own segments

A sign is never scaled or distorted as a whole. Width and stroke are fixed by the column; only specific segments lengthen: straight stems, the counters between arms, the straight parts of bowls, and the diagonals (whose perpendicular stroke stays constant).

Each sign has a height range in which it keeps its construction. Motion and layout always leave every box enough room for its sign; a short canvas makes the mark narrower, never the letters smaller.

04 · LinesDraft

One, two or three lines

Two lines is the reference lockup. One line keeps the boxes still and lets the letters breathe inside them; three lines repeat the reel with an extra box per column. The rules of the letters and the safe area do not change.

05 · PaletteDraft · tints, tones and shades are a proposal

Four primaries, with tints, tones and shades

The artwork uses the four primaries on white, paper, charcoal or ink. Tints (with white), tones (with grey) and shades (with black) organise the interface and secondary material; the tints are also offered as backgrounds.

  • Never place blocks on a background of their own colour: they disappear.
  • No magenta, no gradients, no transparency.
  • Charcoal #40424F for text and lines; white for frame interiors.

06 · Examples of useDraft

Formats for social media

Export from LIVELIVE Lab: PNG at the exact size of the format, or MP4 (H.264, 30 fps, 1–30 s). Pieces can be saved, copied as a code and opened again exactly.

07 · Do and don'tDraft

Keep the construction

Do

  • Keep the four columns and their order.
  • Change box heights, colours by column, lines and formats with the Lab.
  • Keep the safe area free.
  • Use frames with white interiors.

Don't

  • Stretch, squash or rescale letters.
  • Put blocks on a background of the same colour.
  • Add effects, gradients or transparency.
  • Replace the constructed letters with another typeface.
